# Ahmed N. Hassan

**AI Engineer & Researcher · UX Engineer**

I work at the intersection of AI, design, and software engineering, with a focus on building intelligent systems and digital experiences that make sense to people.

I'm currently pursuing a **PhD in Artificial Intelligence** at **Nanjing University of Information Science and Technology (NUIST)**. I previously completed a **Master's in Computer Science and Technology** at NUIST, with a research direction in **AI and natural language processing**.

My interests span **Human–AI Interaction**, **agentic systems**, **human-centered computing**, **UX engineering**, and the connection between visual design and interactive software.
